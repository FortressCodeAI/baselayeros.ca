Pricing Overview

BaseLayerOS uses a transparent, usage‑based pricing model built around GIUs — Governance Integrity Units. GIUs meter deterministic execution, invariant depth, sector overlays, and audit trail generation.

GIU Economics

GIU Price: 0.25 CAD per GIU

Standard Event Burn: 500 GIUs (125 CAD)

Audit Trail Burn: 50 GIUs per trace segment (12.50 CAD)

A typical envelope (validation + invariants + execution + trace) consumes:

  • Validation: 100 GIUs
  • Invariant checks: 200 GIUs
  • Execution: 500 GIUs
  • Trace: 50–150 GIUs

Total: ~850–950 GIUs → ~212–237 CAD per envelope

Sector Multipliers

Sector overlays increase GIU burn due to deeper invariants and compliance requirements:

  • Finance: 1.25×
  • Healthcare: 1.35×
  • AI Safety: 1.50×
  • Multi‑sector: 1.50×
