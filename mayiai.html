May I AI — Partner Governance Engine

May I AI is a governance engine that produces Kali‑standard envelopes describing identity, capability, policy, invariants, and execution plans. BaseLayerOS does not embed governance logic internally—governance engines remain external, modular, and swappable.

Role of May I AI

May I AI provides governance decisions, capability boundaries, and policy constraints. These decisions are expressed through Kali envelopes, which BaseLayerOS validates and enforces deterministically.

What May I AI Provides

  • Identity & role evaluation
  • Capability authorization
  • Policy selection & enforcement logic
  • Invariant requirements
  • Execution plan construction

Integration with BaseLayerOS

May I AI integrates with BaseLayerOS through the Kali Standard Envelope Schema. BaseLayerOS validates envelopes deterministically using rulebook constraints, invariant checks, and cryptographic routing seals.

Integration Flow

  • May I AI generates a Kali envelope
  • Envelope is submitted to BaseLayerOS
  • Schema validation & rulebook checks
  • Invariant evaluation
  • Deterministic execution or refusal
  • Replayable audit trace generated

Why Keep Governance External?

Governance engines evolve rapidly across industries. By keeping governance external, BaseLayerOS ensures modularity, swappability, and sector‑specific flexibility without changing the substrate.

  • Different industries can use different governance engines
  • Policy logic remains independent from deterministic execution
  • Hyperscaler reviewers can validate governance separately
  • No vendor lock‑in

  • Sector‑specific policy engines
