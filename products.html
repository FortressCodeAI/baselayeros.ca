Why GIU‑Metered Pricing?

GIUs meter deterministic execution. GIUs reflect envelope complexity, invariant depth, sector overlays, and audit trail generation — giving enterprises a transparent, usage‑based model instead of opaque “AI credits.”

BaseLayerOS runs at ~$3 CAD per million deterministic events, enabling hyperscaler‑grade governance at near‑zero operational cost.
