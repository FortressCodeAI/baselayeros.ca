Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be granted within 7 days of purchase if all of the following conditions are met:

  • No access key has been activated.
  • No substrate node has been provisioned.
  • No GIU policies have been deployed into production environments.

Non‑Refundable Items

  • Activated access keys and credentials.
  • Enterprise packs after provisioning or deployment has begun.
  • Custom GIU policy authoring, bespoke integrations, or advisory work.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email your request to fortresscodeai@outlook.com with:

  • Your payment reference or PayPal transaction ID.
  • The pack or service purchased.
  • A brief description of the reason for the refund request.

Processing Time

Refunds are processed manually within 3–5 business days after verification. Approved refunds will be issued via the original payment method where possible.

Enterprise Agreements

For enterprise customers with bespoke agreements, refund and commercial terms may be governed by the specific contract in place. In such cases, the contract terms take precedence over this general Refund Policy.
