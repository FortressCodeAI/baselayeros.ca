Governance Engines

BaseLayerOS is governance‑agnostic. It does not embed a governance brain or make policy decisions. Instead, it accepts governance envelopes from external engines—such as May I AI— as long as they conform to the Kali Standard Envelope Schema.

Why Governance Engines Are External

Governance engines evolve rapidly across industries, sectors, and regulatory environments. By keeping governance external and modular, BaseLayerOS ensures:

  • Governance can be swapped without changing the substrate
  • Different industries can use different governance engines
  • Policy logic remains independent from deterministic execution
  • Hyperscaler reviewers can validate governance separately

How Governance Engines Integrate

Governance engines produce envelopes describing:

  • Identity and actor roles
  • Requested capabilities
  • Policies and constraints
  • Invariant requirements
  • Execution plans

BaseLayerOS validates these envelopes deterministically. If all checks pass, the substrate executes the plan. If not, it refuses with a deterministic refusal trace.
