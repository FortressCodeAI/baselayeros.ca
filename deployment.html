Integration Workflow

Integration follows a deterministic, repeatable onboarding process:

  • 1. Governance Engine Selection
    Choose a governance engine (e.g., May I AI) or use your internal policy engine.
  • 2. Envelope Generation
    Governance engine produces Kali‑standard envelopes describing identity, capability, policies, invariants, and execution plans.
  • 3. Substrate Provisioning
    BaseLayerOS is deployed into your environment with kernel rulebook and invariant modules.
  • 4. Envelope Validation
    BaseLayerOS validates envelopes deterministically using schema, rulebook, and invariant checks.
  • 5. Deterministic Execution
    If validated, the substrate executes the plan as a deterministic state transition.
  • 6. Replay & Audit
    Every execution produces a replayable trace for compliance and audit teams.
